Percentages Explained

Four formulas cover almost every percentage question.

Guide · 5 min read · Updated 2026-11-14

What a percent is

Percent means “per hundred”. 35% is 35/100 = 0.35. To convert a decimal to a percentage multiply by 100.

Percent of a number

x% of y = (x ÷ 100) × y. 15% of 240 = 0.15 × 240 = 36.

What percent is one number of another

part ÷ whole × 100. 18 out of 24 is 18 ÷ 24 = 0.75, so 75%.

Percent change

(new − old) ÷ old × 100. From 80 to 92 is 12 ÷ 80 = 0.15, a 15% increase. From 92 to 80 is −12 ÷ 92 ≈ −13.0%, a smaller percentage because the base is larger.

Discounts and tax

A 20% discount on 60 leaves 80% to pay: 60 × 0.8 = 48. Adding 8% tax to 48 gives 48 × 1.08 = 51.84.

Reverse percentages

If 90 is the price after a 20% increase, the original is 90 ÷ 1.2 = 75. Dividing, not subtracting 20% of 90, is the key step.

Common traps

  • Successive changes multiply: +50% then −50% is ×1.5 × 0.5 = ×0.75, a 25% fall.
  • Percentage points differ from percent: 10% to 12% is 2 points but a 20% relative rise.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find 10% quickly?

Move the decimal point one place left: 10% of 85 is 8.5.

Can a percentage be over 100%?

Yes. 150% means one and a half times the original.

Why is percent decrease different from percent increase?

Each uses a different starting value as the base.
