How to Solve Quadratic Equations

Three reliable methods, one worked example each, and a simple rule for choosing between them.

What a quadratic equation is

A quadratic equation has the form ax² + bx + c = 0 with a ≠ 0. Its graph is a parabola, and the solutions (roots) are the x-values where the parabola meets the x-axis. There can be two real roots, one repeated root, or two complex roots.

Method 1: factoring

Move everything to one side, factor, then set each factor to zero. For x² − 5x + 6 = 0, find two numbers that multiply to 6 and add to −5. They are −2 and −3, so (x − 2)(x − 3) = 0 and x = 2 or x = 3.

Factoring is fastest when the roots are whole numbers or simple fractions. If you cannot spot the factors in about a minute, switch method.

Method 2: completing the square

Take x² + 6x + 2 = 0. Move the constant: x² + 6x = −2. Add (6 ÷ 2)² = 9 to both sides: x² + 6x + 9 = 7, so (x + 3)² = 7. Then x + 3 = ±√7 and x = −3 ± √7. This method also produces the vertex form of the parabola.

Method 3: the quadratic formula

For any quadratic, x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) ÷ 2a. Take 2x² + 3x − 2 = 0, so a = 2, b = 3, c = −2. The discriminant is 9 + 16 = 25, so x = (−3 ± 5) ÷ 4. That gives x = 1/2 or x = −2.

Which method should you use?

  • Try factoring first when the numbers are small.
  • Use completing the square when you need vertex form, or when b is even and a = 1.
  • Use the formula whenever the first two stall. It always works.

Check your answer

Substitute each root back into the original equation. You can also type the equation into the quadratic equation calculator to compare, and open the working to see where your own steps differ.

Frequently asked questions

Can a quadratic equation have only one solution?

Yes. When the discriminant is zero the two roots coincide, for example x² − 6x + 9 = 0 has x = 3 only.

What if a = 0?

Then the x² term disappears and the equation is linear, not quadratic.

Why does the formula have ±?

Because a square root has two values, one positive and one negative, which give the two roots.
