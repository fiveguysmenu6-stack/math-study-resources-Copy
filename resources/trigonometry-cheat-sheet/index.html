Identities

sin²θ + cos²θ = 1tan θ = sin θ ÷ cos θ
sin 2θ = 2 sin θ cos θcos 2θ = cos²θ − sin²θ
sin(A+B) = sin A cos B + cos A sin Bcos(A+B) = cos A cos B − sin A sin B

Conversion and signs

Degrees to radians: multiply by π/180. Radians to degrees: multiply by 180/π. Sine is positive in quadrants I and II, cosine in I and IV, tangent in I and III.
