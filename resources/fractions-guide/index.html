Fractions Made Simple

The four operations, how to simplify, and the mistakes to watch for.

Parts of a fraction

In 3/4 the numerator (3) says how many parts you have and the denominator (4) says how many equal parts make a whole. A fraction is also a division: 3/4 = 3 ÷ 4 = 0.75.

Simplifying

Divide the top and bottom by their greatest common factor. For 18/24 the GCF is 6, so 18/24 = 3/4. Use the GCF and LCM calculator if the factor is not obvious.

Adding and subtracting

The denominators must match. For 3/4 + 1/6 use 12 as the common denominator: 9/12 + 2/12 = 11/12. Only the numerators are added; the denominator stays the same.

Multiplying

Multiply across: 2/3 × 3/5 = 6/15 = 2/5. You can cancel first: the 3 on top and the 3 on the bottom cancel, leaving 2/5 straight away.

Dividing

Flip the second fraction and multiply: 2/3 ÷ 4/5 = 2/3 × 5/4 = 10/12 = 5/6.

Mixed numbers

Convert to improper fractions first: 2 1/3 = 7/3. To go back, divide: 11/4 = 2 remainder 3, so 2 3/4.

Common mistakes

  • Adding denominators: 1/2 + 1/3 is not 2/5. It is 5/6.
  • Forgetting to flip when dividing.
  • Leaving the answer unsimplified.

Practise in the fraction calculator, which shows every step. For algebraic fractions, the Mathvelo math solver follows the same ideas with variables.

Frequently asked questions

How do I compare two fractions?

Give them a common denominator and compare numerators, or convert both to decimals.

Is 0.75 the same as 3/4?

Yes. Every fraction has a decimal form; this one terminates after two digits.

What is the reciprocal of a fraction?

Swap the numerator and denominator. The reciprocal of 2/3 is 3/2.
