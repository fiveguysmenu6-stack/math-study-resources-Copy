Exponent Calculator

Evaluate aᵇ for any base and exponent (negative, zero, fractional or large), simplify expressions using the laws of exponents, convert numbers to scientific notation, solve equations such as 2ˣ = 8 and convert between radicals and exponents.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter leaves your device.

How to use the exponent calculator

  1. Pick a tab: Evaluate, Simplify with laws, Scientific notation, Solve, Radical ↔ exponent, Logarithms or Table of powers.
  2. Type your numbers and press Calculate.
  3. Open the working to see which law was used.

The method behind it

Laws: aᵐ · aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ; aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ; (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ; (ab)ⁿ = aⁿbⁿ; a⁻ⁿ = 1 ÷ aⁿ; a⁰ = 1 (a ≠ 0); a^(1/n) = ⁿ√a.

Worked example

Worked example: 2¹⁰ = 1024. 8^(2/3) = (³√8)² = 2² = 4. 5⁻² = 1/25 = 0.04.

Frequently asked questions

Why is anything to the power 0 equal to 1?

Because aⁿ ÷ aⁿ = aⁿ⁻ⁿ = a⁰, and any non-zero number divided by itself is 1.

What does a negative exponent mean?

It means the reciprocal: a⁻ⁿ = 1/aⁿ.

How do I write a number in scientific notation?

Move the decimal point until one non-zero digit is in front, and count the moves: 45,000 = 4.5 × 10⁴.
