Law of Cosines Calculator

Know two sides and the angle between them? Find the third side, both other angles, the area and a drawing. Tabs also cover three known sides (SSS), two sides with a non-included angle (SSA) and the distance between two points.

How to use the law of cosines calculator

  1. Choose SAS, SSS or SSA.
  2. Enter the known sides and angles.
  3. Press Calculate to see the missing parts, the area and the working.

The method behind it

Law of cosines: c² = a² + b² − 2ab cos C. Rearranged, cos C = (a² + b² − c²) ÷ 2ab. When C = 90° it reduces to the Pythagorean theorem. SSA can have zero, one or two valid triangles (the ambiguous case).

Worked example

Worked example: a = 5, b = 7 and C = 60°. c² = 25 + 49 − 2(5)(7)cos 60° = 74 − 35 = 39, so c ≈ 6.245.

Frequently asked questions

When should I use the law of cosines instead of the law of sines?

Use cosines for SAS and SSS. Use sines when you know an angle and its opposite side.

What is the ambiguous case?

With SSA the given information can fit two different triangles, one, or none.

Does it work for right triangles?

Yes. With a 90° angle the term 2ab cos C becomes 0 and you get c² = a² + b².
