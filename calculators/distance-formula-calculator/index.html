Distance Formula Calculator

Find the distance between two points in two or three dimensions with exact radicals, together with the midpoint and slope. Other tabs find a missing coordinate, the distance from a point to a line, between parallel lines, from a point to a circle, triangle properties and polygon area.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter leaves your device.

How to use the distance formula calculator

  1. Choose 2D distance, 3D distance or another tab.
  2. Enter the coordinates and press Calculate.
  3. Read the exact and decimal distance and the working.

The method behind it

2D: d = √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²). 3D: d = √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)² + (z₂ − z₁)²). Midpoint: ((x₁ + x₂) ÷ 2, (y₁ + y₂) ÷ 2). The formula is the Pythagorean theorem applied to the horizontal and vertical differences.

Worked example

Worked example: (1, 2) and (4, 6). The differences are 3 and 4, so d = √(9 + 16) = 5. The midpoint is (2.5, 4).

Frequently asked questions

How is the distance formula related to the Pythagorean theorem?

The horizontal and vertical differences are the two legs of a right triangle, and the distance is the hypotenuse.

Does the order of the points matter?

No. The differences are squared, so the distance is the same either way.

Can I find the distance from a point to a line?

Yes, use the Point to line tab.
