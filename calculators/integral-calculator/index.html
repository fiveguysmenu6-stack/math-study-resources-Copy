Integral Calculator

Find the antiderivative with the rule used at every step, plus a derivative check, or evaluate a definite integral between two limits. Type the integrand using ^ for powers.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter leaves your device.

How to use the integral calculator

  1. Choose Indefinite integral or Definite integral.
  2. Type the integrand, for example x^2, and the limits if needed.
  3. Press Calculate and read the steps.

The method behind it

Power rule: ∫ xⁿ dx = xⁿ⁺¹ ÷ (n + 1) + C for n ≠ −1. Fundamental theorem: ∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx = F(b) − F(a), where F is an antiderivative of f. Harder integrals use substitution or integration by parts.

Worked example

Worked example: ∫₀³ x² dx = [x³ ÷ 3] from 0 to 3 = 27 ÷ 3 − 0 = 9.

Frequently asked questions

Why is there a +C?

Derivatives of constants are zero, so an antiderivative is only defined up to a constant.

How do I know the answer is right?

Differentiate it. You should get the original function back, which is the check the calculator shows.

What does a definite integral measure?

The signed area between the curve and the x-axis from a to b.
