Percentage Calculator

Pick the question you are asking, fill in the blanks of the sentence and the answer appears with a bar, the formula and each step. It covers percent of a number, what percent one number is of another, percent change, discounts and tax, tips, margin and markup, and reverse percentages.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter leaves your device.

How to use the percentage calculator

  1. Choose a question type, for example What is X% of Y or Percent change.
  2. Type your numbers into the blanks. Decimals are fine.
  3. Press Calculate to see the answer, the formula and the working.
  4. Use the More tools tab for extra percentage tools such as grades, VAT and interest.

The method behind it

Percent of a number: x% of y = (x ÷ 100) × y.
Percent change: (new − old) ÷ old × 100.
Increase by p%: multiply by (1 + p/100). Decrease by p%: multiply by (1 − p/100).
Reverse percentage: original = final ÷ (1 ± p/100).

Worked example

Worked example: 25% of 80 = 0.25 × 80 = 20. A price rising from 80 to 92 is a change of (92 − 80) ÷ 80 = 0.15, so +15%. A coat reduced by 15% from 60 costs 60 × 0.85 = 51. If a price is 90 after a 20% rise, the original was 90 ÷ 1.2 = 75.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between percent change and percentage points?

If a rate moves from 10% to 12% it rose by 2 percentage points, which is a 20% relative increase. The calculator has a dedicated mode for this.

How do I find the original price after a discount?

Divide the sale price by (1 − discount). A 30% sale price of 70 means the original was 70 ÷ 0.7 = 100.

Why is a 50% increase followed by a 50% decrease not back to the start?

The second percentage applies to a larger number. 100 × 1.5 × 0.5 = 75.
