Fraction Calculator

Type a fraction expression such as 1/2 + 3/4 or a mixed number like 2 1/3 − 1 3/4 and see the answer, a pie model and each step. Tabs let you calculate, convert, compare and simplify, and the More tools tab covers further fraction modes.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter leaves your device.

How to use the fraction calculator

  1. Choose Calculate, Convert, Compare or Simplify.
  2. Type your fractions. Mixed numbers (2 1/3), decimals, powers and brackets are accepted.
  3. Read the answer in simplest form, as a mixed number and as a decimal, then open the steps.
  4. Tap an example chip to see how a typical problem is laid out.

The method behind it

Add or subtract: rewrite with a common denominator, then combine the numerators. Multiply: multiply numerators and denominators. Divide: multiply by the reciprocal of the second fraction. Simplify: divide the numerator and denominator by their greatest common factor.

Worked example

Worked example: 3/4 + 1/6. The common denominator is 12, so 9/12 + 2/12 = 11/12. And 2/3 ÷ 4/5 = 2/3 × 5/4 = 10/12 = 5/6.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add fractions with different denominators?

Find a common denominator (the least common multiple is neatest), convert each fraction, then add the numerators.

How do I turn an improper fraction into a mixed number?

Divide the numerator by the denominator. The quotient is the whole part and the remainder is the new numerator: 11/4 = 2 3/4.

Is the answer always fully simplified?

Yes. The result is reduced by the greatest common factor of numerator and denominator.
