Slope Calculator

Pick a tab, type your numbers and press Calculate. You get the slope, the line equation in three forms, both intercepts, the angle and the percent grade. Other tabs read a slope from an equation, check parallel or perpendicular lines, find a missing coordinate, test collinear points and compute average rate of change.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter leaves your device.

How to use the slope calculator

  1. Choose a tab, for example Two points.
  2. Enter (x₁, y₁) and (x₂, y₂).
  3. Press Calculate to see the slope, the equation and the working.

The method behind it

Slope: m = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁), the rise over the run. Point-slope form: y − y₁ = m(x − x₁). Slope-intercept form: y = mx + b. Parallel lines have equal slopes; perpendicular lines have slopes that multiply to −1. A vertical line has an undefined slope.

Worked example

Worked example: the points (1, 2) and (5, 10). m = (10 − 2) ÷ (5 − 1) = 8 ÷ 4 = 2. The line is y − 2 = 2(x − 1), which simplifies to y = 2x.

Frequently asked questions

What does a negative slope mean?

The line falls as you move to the right.

What is the slope of a horizontal line?

Zero, because the rise is zero. A vertical line has an undefined slope.

How do I find a perpendicular slope?

Take the negative reciprocal. A line with slope 2 has a perpendicular slope of −1/2.
