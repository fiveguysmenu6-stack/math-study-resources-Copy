Scientific Calculator

A full scientific calculator in your browser. It follows the layout of a Casio fx-991 class handheld: SHIFT and ALPHA key layers, natural fraction display, exact answers such as 2π or 1/2, and a side panel with a step-by-step solver, graphing, history, variables and notebook.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter leaves your device.

How to use the scientific calculator

  1. Type with the keypad or your keyboard. 7*8+2^2 then Enter gives 60.
  2. Use the fraction key or type 1/3+1/6 to see an exact answer (1/2). The S⇔D key switches between fraction and decimal.
  3. Check the DEG label in the top status line before using trig. Open the settings key at the top right to switch between degrees and radians.
  4. Open the Solver tab for words and letters (for example Solve x^2-5x+6=0), Graph to plot functions, History to reuse earlier work and Modes for equation, matrix, vector, statistics, complex, base-N and table tools.

The method behind it

The calculator follows the standard order of operations: brackets first, then powers and roots, then multiplication and division, then addition and subtraction. Trigonometric inputs are read in the angle unit shown in the status line, so sin(30) is 1/2 in degree mode and about −0.988 in radian mode. Constants such as π and e are kept exact until you ask for a decimal.

Worked example

Try: sin(30) in degree mode shows 1/2. log(1000) shows 3. 5! shows 120. 2^10 shows 1024. pi*2 shows 2π.

Frequently asked questions

Is this calculator accurate enough for exams?

It gives exact fractions and radicals where a handheld would, and double-precision decimals otherwise. Always check which calculators your exam board allows, because some tests restrict graphing or algebra features.

How do I switch between degrees and radians?

The current unit is shown in the status line above the display (DEG or RAD). Use the settings key at the top right of the keypad to change it.

Does it save my history?

Yes. The History tab keeps recent calculations in this browser only. Nothing is sent to a server.
