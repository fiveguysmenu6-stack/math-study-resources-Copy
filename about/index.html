About Math Study Resources

Math Study Resources is a small collection of free math calculators and study guides. It is built as a static site: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no account, no server code and no tracking scripts.

Where the calculators come from

The tools are adapted from the calculators on Mathvelo, a free math site. Each calculator here runs completely in your browser. The online AI features of the originals (the AI solver and photo scanner) are not part of this site, because they need a server. The built-in step-by-step working that each calculator computes locally is unchanged. If you want AI help, the Free AI Math Solver and the full Online Scientific Calculator are available on Mathvelo.

Privacy

What you type into a calculator stays on your device. The only things stored are your theme choice and, in the basic calculator, its history, both in your browser's local storage. Clearing site data removes them.

Accuracy

Results are computed with double-precision arithmetic, and exact fractions or radicals are shown where the calculator supports them. Check important results independently, and confirm which calculators your exam board allows.
